# MAKHEX COMMERCE CHARTER — OWNER DIRECTION (2026-10-27)

This is a permanent acceptance criterion for anyone touching the store.

1. **Showcase maker projects first.** The project gallery, instructions, media, original CAD, reproducible BOM and complete build kit are the value of MAKHEX.
2. **One MAKHEX checkout.** Buyers add a single component or an entire kit, remain in the MAKHEX-branded sales experience and submit ONE order. They do not have to visit 15 external suppliers.
3. **MAKHEX is seller of record.** Purchases and prices are presented as MAKHEX's, the buyer's transaction is with MAKHEX through its supported Squarespace Payments checkout, and fulfillment is MAKHEX's responsibility through Doroppu or manual procurement.
4. **Source links are INTERNAL by default.** A buyer-facing external supplier comparison is a contingency when MAKHEX cannot fill that item, never the primary CTA, sidebar or cart content.
5. **Owner approves EXCEPTIONS, not normal orders.** Examples: source variant mismatch, unavailable inventory, shipping cost beyond margin, custom HAT/IMU/printed components, alternate parts, refund/partial shipment and uncertain supplier placement.
6. **Supplier prices do NOT become retail guesses.** Current verified landed supplier cost × 2.5, free U.S. shipping subject to margin; no stale historical price, unrelated PCB variant or made-up price.
7. **Payments follow real authorization.** Until matching supplier stock, SKUs, payment, tax, return, and delivery capability are verified, the buyer can place a single first-party order request, but no card is charged. Owner resolves exceptions, then attaches a real Squarespace Pay Link. Never claim a submitted request is a paid purchase.
8. **Never bypass fulfillment safety.** Do not automatically retry uncertain supplier orders. No silent price changes or undisclosed substitutions, no buyer card data on Cloudflare, no speculative shipping dates.
9. **SKU completeness:** 30 unique MAKHEX catalog components across all 12 builds, plus the correctly handled Mosaico module-versus-bundle alternative. A single part or full build must remain in one MAKHEX cart through checkout.
10. **Permanent regression rule:** tests should fail when the cart's primary CTA becomes "compare external sellers", when a build routes to a supplier checkout, or when exceptions are silently approved.
11. **Future direct payment path:** the automatic trusted-case route can open MAKHEX's own Squarespace shop.makhex.com hosted checkout only with verified cart/variant/stock; otherwise exception review and order-specific hosted payment are required.

This charter supersedes older "shop is only a parts research comparison site" copy. It does not authorize charging cards or placing supplier purchases without a verified payment/fulfillment workflow. Keep the site on Squarespace Basic while possible.
